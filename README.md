## What is ShareList?
ShareList is a way for people to make a playlist of anything, and easily share them; all with no sign ups.

<img width="1913" height="947" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1ea92469-97cc-46cc-aa77-07bc0c6956cf" />

Do you have a collection of websites you would like to make a list of for later? You got ShareList! Do  you want to make a playlist that has content scretching from Youtube to Spotify? You got ShareList! Do you just need to put a whole lot of stuff together for safe keeping? **You Got Share List**...

Basically ShareList is a great tool for use cases that vary. I hope you enjoy!!
