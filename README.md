## What is ShareList?
ShareList is a way for people to make a playlist of anything, and easily share them; all with no sign ups.

<img width="1913" height="947" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1ea92469-97cc-46cc-aa77-07bc0c6956cf" />

Do you have a collection of websites you would like to make a list of for later? You got ShareList! Do  you want to make a playlist that has content scretching from Youtube to Spotify? You got ShareList! Do you just need to put a whole lot of stuff together for safe keeping? **You Got Share List**...

Basically ShareList is a great tool for use cases that vary. I hope you enjoy!!

## How to Use ShareList?

For many use cases, ShareList is relatively simple! All you need to do is go to the ShareList website:

[![HERE](https://img.shields.io/badge/HERE-sharelist.krambo.space-blue?style=for-the-badge)](https://sharelist.krambo.space/)

After:
1. Click "Create Playlist" or the "Create" menu text located on the top left.
2. Fill in the "Name" field. **REQUIRED**
3. Fill in the "Description" field.
4. Pick an icon or use the default "share" icon.
5. Set an expiration date. **REQUIRED**
> [!NOTE]  
> Can not be set to before or the date of when the playlist is being created, or past 90 days of when the playlist is being created.
6. Set a password **REQUIRED**
> [!NOTE]  
> Minimum length of four characters.
7. Click "Submit"
8. It will automatically redirect you to the playlist page.
9. Click "Edit"
10. Click the "Add" button that shows up to make a new playlist entry.
<details>
    <summary>Editing an Entry</summary>
    <p>1. Click the pencil icon to start editing that specific entry.</p>
    <p>2. Change the link field(by default is "about:blank") to the link you want that specific entry to open.</p>
    <p>3. Click the floppy disk icon to save</p>
    <p>NOTE: The entry does not save until the full playlist is saved via entering a password.</p>
</details>

> [!NOTE]  
> Click the trash icon located next to an entry to delete that specific entry. Does not apply until entire playlist is saved via entering a password.
11. Edit any other information like the title and description of the playlist if needed.
12. Click the "Save" button that now is in place of the previous "Edit" button.
13. Enter the password of the project to confirm changes and click the small arrow.
14. Optionally, click "Share Playlist", which will either open up a share menu on the bottom(for devices like phones that have the feature) or copy the link, and say "Link Copied!" to signal that the link is now in your clipboard.
> [!WARNING]  
> ShareList uses a very simple form of security. Your created playlists are available across the internet and can be accessed by anyone if they have the link. Do not entrust ShareList with any personal or important information that you would rather keep secret.
